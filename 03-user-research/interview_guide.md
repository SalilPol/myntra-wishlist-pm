# Interview guide (25 minutes, semi-structured)

Ask the participant to share their screen with the Myntra wishlist open. The wishlist is the
stimulus. Do not ask "why haven't you bought it"; ask what would have to be true.

## 0. Warm-up (2 min)
- How often do you open Myntra? What usually brings you in?

## 1. Wishlist as an object (4 min)
- Scroll through your wishlist. How does it feel to look at it?
- When did you last open it on your own, without a notification?
- Is everything here something you want to own, or are some of these something else? (Probe: inspiration, "someday", saved for someone else, waiting for sale.)

## 2. Item walk-through: pick 3 items, most recent first (10 min)
For each:
- What made you save this? What were you doing at that moment?
- Did you intend to buy it that day? That week?
- Do you still want it? What has changed?
- **What would have to be true for you to buy it today?** (Sit in the silence.)
- What do you still not know about it? (Probe: size, how it looks on you, fabric, whether it goes with anything, whether it is worth the price.)
- Have you looked at anything else instead? Where?
- Did you show it to anyone or ask anyone? How? (Probe: screenshot, WhatsApp, in person.)
- Have you checked it on another app or in a store?
- Is there a date by which this stops mattering?

## 3. Workarounds (4 min)
- Last time you were unsure about size, what did you do?
- Have you ever ordered two sizes? What happened?
- What do you do when a saved item shows "size unavailable"?
- What do you do when you are torn between two similar items?

## 4. Closing (3 min)
- If Myntra could do one thing to help you decide on the items you actually want, what would it be?
- Anything you wish the wishlist did that it does not?
