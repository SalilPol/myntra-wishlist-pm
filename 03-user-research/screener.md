# Interview screener

Target segment (chosen from engine ranking plus decomposition logic, see 04-problem-definition.md):

**Women, 24 to 32, metro or tier-1 city, repeat Myntra buyers (3+ orders in the last 12 months),
with 15+ items on their wishlist, who saved at least one apparel item priced Rs 800 to 2,500 in
the last 60 days and have not bought it.**

Why this segment: highest wishlist volume per user, apparel carries the highest fit and look
uncertainty, this price band is where "just order it and return" stops being casual, and the
engine's social-validation and fit signals cluster here.

## Screener questions (Google Form or WhatsApp)

1. Have you shopped on Myntra in the last 12 months? How many orders, roughly? [need 3+]
2. Roughly how many items are on your Myntra wishlist right now? [need 15+]
3. Did you save any clothing item priced between Rs 800 and 2,500 in the last two months that you have not bought yet? [need yes]
4. Age band: 18 to 23 / 24 to 32 / 33+ [need 24 to 32]
5. City [need metro or tier-1]
6. Would you be willing to open your wishlist on a 25-minute video call and talk through it? [need yes]

Recruit 8, expect 6 to show. No compensation; offer to share the findings.

## Where to recruit

Building or society WhatsApp groups, office colleagues (not direct reports), spouse's network,
r/IndianFashionAddicts (ask mods first), college alumni groups. Avoid people who know what
you are building.
